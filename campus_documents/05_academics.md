## Section 2: Credit System & CGPA Grading Policy
- **Semester Credit Structure**: Each semester carries **15 to 20 credits** across lectures, practical labs, and project work.
- **CGPA Calculation Formula**: Standard weighted average CGPA formula:
  $$\text{CGPA} = \frac{\sum (\text{Course Credits} \times \text{Grade Points Obtained})}{\sum \text{Total Registered Credits}}$$

- **Even Semester (Semesters II, IV, VI, VIII)**: February to July.
