# MITS Student Code of Conduct & Discipline Policy
> **Context Summary:** Official student code of conduct, anti-ragging helpline, attendance discipline, dress code regulations, and grievance redressal process for Madanapalle Institute of Technology & Science (MITS).

## Section 1: Student Code of Conduct
- Use of mobile phones is restricted during lectures and examinations 
- Damage to college property may result in disciplinary action and/or fines 
## Section 2: Anti-Ragging Policy & Zero Tolerance
- **Policy**: MITS enforces a strict zero-tolerance policy against ragging in any form on campus or inside hostels.
- **National Anti-Ragging Helpline (UGC, India)**: 1800-180-5522 (toll-free, 24/7) — this is India's official UGC national helpline, applicable to all colleges, not MITS-specific.
- **National Anti-Ragging Helpline Email**: helpline@antiragging.in
- **MITS Campus Anti-Ragging Committee Contact**: 9123456789

## Section 3: Attendance Discipline Policy
- **Minimum Requirement**: 75% attendance is strictly enforced across all academic courses for exam eligibility.

## Section 4: Dress Code Guidelines
- Students are expected to wear neat, presentable attire on campus; formal wear may be required during exams, seminars, or official college events 

## Section 5: Grievance Redressal Cell
- Students can raise academic or personal grievances through the college's Grievance Redressal Cell
