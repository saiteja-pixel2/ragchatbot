# MITS College Admissions Guide & Application Guidelines (2026-27)
> **Context Summary:** Official admission eligibility, AP EAPCET entrance exam criteria, Category-B management quota guidelines, required certificates, and Admissions Office contact information for Madanapalle Institute of Technology & Science (MITS).

## Section 1: Admission Overview & Branch Offerings
Madanapalle Institute of Technology & Science (MITS) offers undergraduate B.Tech programs in CSE (AI & ML), CSE (Data Science), CSE, ECE, EEE, Civil, and Mechanical Engineering, along with postgraduate MCA, MBA, and M.Tech degrees.

## Section 2: Eligibility Criteria
- **Undergraduate (B.Tech)**: Candidates must have passed Intermediate (12th Grade / 10+2) examination from a recognized Board (BIEAP / CBSE / ICSE). There is no specific additional eligibility cutoff beyond passing Intermediate.
- **Postgraduate (MCA / MBA / M.Tech)**: Candidates must possess an appropriate Bachelor's degree from a recognized university.

## Section 3: Entrance Examinations Accepted
- **B.Tech Convener Quota**: AP EAPCET (Engineering, Agriculture and Pharmacy Common Entrance Test). Counseling Code: `MITS`.
- **MCA / MBA Convener Quota**: AP ICET.
- **B.Tech Lateral Entry**: AP ECET.
- **Management Quota (Category-B)**: Direct admission based on Intermediate merit marks and JEE percentile.

## Section 4: Required Certificates & Documents for Verification
Students must submit original and two sets of self-attested photocopies of the following documents during admission:
1. Intermediate / 10+2 Marks Memo & Pass Certificate.
2. SSC / 10th Class Marks Memorandum.
3. Transfer Certificate (TC) from the last attended institution.
4. Aadhaar Card photocopy (Student and Parent).
5. Study & Conduct Certificates (6th Grade to Intermediate).
6. Income Certificate (issued by Meeseva / Tahsildar for fee reimbursement eligibility).
7. Integrated Caste Certificate (if applicable).
8. AP EAPCET Rank Card & Hall Ticket.
