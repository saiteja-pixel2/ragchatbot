## Section 2: Scholarship Schemes & Concessions
- **Jagananna Vidya Deevena (JVD)**: AP Government full tuition fee reimbursement scheme. JVD covers **tuition fee only**. JVD does **NOT** cover hostel or mess fees.
- **Vasathi Deevena**: Not available at MITS.
- **JEE Percentile Merit Scholarship**: If a student secures a **JEE percentile above 80**, their annual tuition fee is reduced by **30%**. This merit concession applies across every branch (CSE, CSE AI & ML, CSE Data Science, ECE, EEE, Civil, Mechanical).

## Section 3: Fee Refund & Withdrawal Policy
- **Refund Policy**: **No refunds under any circumstances**. Once paid, tuition, admission, or hostel fees will not be refunded or transferred upon seat cancellation or withdrawal.
