# MITS Student Clubs, Technical Societies & Annual Events (2026-27)
> **Context Summary:** Active technical societies (IEEE, ACM, CSI), cultural clubs (music, dance, drama, fine arts), sports clubs, annual fests, student council, and registration process via in-person visit and Google Form for MITS.

## Section 1: Active Student Clubs & Societies
- **Technical Societies**: IEEE Student Branch, ACM Student Chapter, and Computer Society of India (CSI).
- **Cultural Clubs**: Music Club, Dance Club, Drama & Theatre Society, and Fine Arts Club.
- **Sports Clubs**: Cricket Club and Football Club.

## Section 2: How to Join a Club
- **Registration Process**: Students can join any club by visiting the club coordinator in person at the college campus and filling out the official registration **Google Form**.

## Section 3: Student Council & Governance
- **Student Council**: The student governing body represents student welfare. Students can join the Student Council by submitting an official application registration form during the annual selection drive.
