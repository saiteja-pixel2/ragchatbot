# MITS Examination Regulations, Attendance & Revaluation Rules (2026-27)
> **Context Summary:** Evaluation marks distribution (40 internal / 60 external), 75% attendance rule, 65-74% medical condonation, ₹500 revaluation fee (10-day window), backlog exam schedules, and online result publication for MITS.

## Section 1: Evaluation Marks Distribution
Each academic course is evaluated for a total of **100 marks**:
- **Internal Continuous Assessment**: **40 Marks** (based on mid-term exams, quizzes, lab evaluations, and assignments).
- **External Semester End Examination**: **60 Marks** (conducted at the end of each semester).

## Section 2: Attendance Requirements & Medical Condonation
- **Minimum Requirement**: Students must maintain at least **75% attendance** in each registered course to be eligible to sit for semester end examinations.
- **Medical Condonation**: Students maintaining between **65% and 74% attendance** may apply for medical condonation by submitting verified medical certificates to the Academic Dean.

## Section 3: Revaluation & Recounting Process
- **Revaluation Fee**: **₹500 per course**.
- **Application Window**: Revaluation applications must be submitted online on the college portal within **10 calendar days** of official result declaration.

## Section 4: Backlog Exams & Result Declaration
- **Backlog Exam Schedule**: Supplementary / backlog examinations are conducted immediately **after main semester examinations conclude**.
- **Result Publication**: All exam results, SGPA, and CGPA mark sheets are officially published online on the official MITS college website.
