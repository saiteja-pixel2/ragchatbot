# MITS Hostel Accommodation, Mess & Residential Policy (2026-27)
> **Context Summary:** Official residential hostel accommodation details, room options (Single AC & Double Room), mess menu, 10 PM curfew, zero-extra-charge Wi-Fi/laundry, visitor rules, and leave policy for MITS campus hostels.

## Section 1: Hostel Blocks & Room Categories
MITS maintains separate Boys' Hostels and Girls' Hostels located directly inside the campus perimeter. Only two room types are available:
- **Single Occupancy AC Room**: ₹1,80,000 per academic year.
- **Double Occupancy Room**: ₹1,20,000 per academic year.
- *Note*: No other room types (triple/quad) are offered.

## Section 2: Mess Charges & Meal Timings
- **Mess Fee**: ₹40,000 per academic year (already **included** in the overall hostel fee structure).
- **Meal Service**: Three meals served daily (Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner) in the central hostel dining hall.

## Section 3: Amenities & Included Services
- **Wi-Fi 6 Internet**: High-speed wireless internet available across all rooms at **no extra charge**.
- **Laundry Facilities**: Washing machine access and laundry service included at **no extra charge**.

## Section 4: Hostel Rules, Curfew & Visitor Policy
- **Hostel Curfew**: All hostellers must return to their respective hostel blocks by **10:00 PM** sharp every night.


- **Leave Policy**: Students can leave campus directly without undergoing a formal written leave application process.
- **Visitor Policy**: Only verified family members and friends are allowed to visit hostellers in designated visitor lounge areas.
