# MITS Campus Facilities, Library, Transport & Compute Labs (2026-27)
> **Context Summary:** Central Library timings & borrowing rules (3 books / 10 days), 8x NVIDIA H100 GPU AI Lab, sports grounds (cricket, volleyball, football, basketball, gym), 9am-5pm canteen, 24/7 medical center, and bus transport details for MITS.

## Section 1: Central Library Hours & Borrowing Privileges
- **Operating Hours**:
  - **Monday to Saturday**: 8:00 AM to 11:00 PM.
  - **Sunday**: 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM.
- **Borrowing Privileges**: Students can borrow up to **3 books** simultaneously for a duration of up to **10 days**.

## Section 2: High-Performance Compute (HPC) NVIDIA GPU AI Lab
- **Infrastructure**: Equipped with **8x NVIDIA H100 GPU** node clusters for deep learning, AI research, and computer vision capstone projects.

## Section 3: Campus Sports & Physical Recreation Facilities
MITS offers outdoor grounds and indoor sports facilities:
- **Outdoor Sports**: Cricket ground, Football field, Volleyball courts, and Basketball courts.
- **Indoor Games**: Carrom, Chess, Ludo, and a fully equipped Student Gym.

## Section 4: Canteen, Medical & Transport Facilities
- **Canteen Hours**: Open daily from **9:00 AM to 5:00 PM**.
- **Medical Health Center**: **Available 24/7** with resident medical officer and first-aid facility.
- **Bus Transport**:venaktesulu
